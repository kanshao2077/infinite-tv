# INFINITE TV｜作品说明

1999 — ∞

原点的缺席

THE ABSENCE OF AN ORIGIN

一次关于观看、递归与未完成未来的媒介实验。

A media experiment in looking, recursion, and an unfinished future.

千禧年并未成为过去。它作为一个未被兑现的未来，滞留在媒介内部。天线仍然指向天空，接收的却不再是远方，而是接收这一动作本身。

The millennium has never become the past. It remains lodged inside the medium as a future never fulfilled. The antennas still point toward the sky, receiving no longer a distant world, but the act of reception itself.

在这间被反复显影的客厅里，屏幕不再充当世界的窗口。它撤销了内外之分：每一个内部，都只是尚未被识别的外部；每一次抵达，都以另一层界面为代价。

In this endlessly developing room, the screen ceases to be a window onto the world. It dissolves the distinction between inside and outside: every interior is an exterior not yet recognized; every arrival exacts another interface.

观看者以为自己正向原点逼近。原点却只能通过持续后退，维持它作为原点的地位。递归由此成为一种时间政治：未来被允许无限接近，却从不获得发生的时刻。

The viewer believes they are approaching an origin. Yet the origin can retain its status only by continually receding. Recursion becomes a politics of time: the future is permitted infinite proximity, but never the moment in which to occur.

“最后一台电视”并非藏在序列尽头的某件物。它是观看为自身制造的终止条件——当你停止寻找，终点才被短暂地生产出来。

The last television is no object concealed at the end of the sequence. It is a condition of termination that looking invents for itself. Only when you stop searching is an endpoint briefly brought into being.

世界退场之后，观看仍在继续。

After the world withdraws, looking continues.

一则媒介寓言

THE ORIGIN IS ALWAYS ELSEWHERE.
